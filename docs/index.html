Why ANT over the alternatives?

Most AI note-takers charge monthly fees and send your data to the cloud. ANT is different.

01

Everything runs locally

Transcription happens on your GPU via faster-whisper. No audio ever uploaded to a server. Otter, Fireflies, and Fathom all require cloud — ANT does not.

02

Bring your own AI keys

Use any provider you trust. No vendor lock-in. No ANT subscription. You pay only for what you use — and you can always fall back to free Ollama models.

03

Stealth mode that actually works

ANT uses Windows DRM APIs (SetWindowDisplayAffinity) — the same tech Netflix uses to block screen recording. Competitors can't offer this.

04

Built for job seekers

Mock interviews, resume review, job tracker, pre-interview prep, and cognitive graph — tools specifically designed to help you land your next role.

05

Race mode: ask all AIs at once

ANT queries every configured AI provider simultaneously and streams the first response. Get answers faster than any single-provider tool.

06

100% free. Forever. No catch.

MIT Licensed. No premium tiers. No "unlimited" upsells. No telemetry. We don't want your money — we want AI tools to be accessible to everyone.
