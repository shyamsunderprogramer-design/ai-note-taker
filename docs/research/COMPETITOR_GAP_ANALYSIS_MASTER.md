## Executive Summary

AI Note Taker has achieved **competitive parity or superiority** across most feature categories compared to premium tools ($25-$800/mo). The app currently matches or exceeds competitors in:

- ✅ **Multi-provider AI support** (8+ providers - unique advantage)
- ✅ **Document RAG** (feature most competitors lack)
- ✅ **Vision/Screenshot capabilities** (unique advantage)
- ✅ **Editable summaries** (unique advantage)
- ✅ **Export flexibility** (MD/JSON/TXT - best in class)
- ✅ **Analytics depth** (speaker ratios, daily trends - best in class)
- ✅ **CRM integration** (only tool with generic webhook)
- ✅ **Privacy** (local processing + open source - best in class)
- ✅ **Price** (FREE vs $20-$800/mo)

**Key Gaps Identified:** 18 features across competitors, prioritized by impact.

## Unique Opportunities (Beyond Gaps)

### 1. **Open-Source AI Voice Agent**
MeetGeek's AI Voice Agents are proprietary and expensive ($59/mo). An **open-source voice agent** would be:
- First of its kind
- Massive community interest
- Differentiation from all competitors

### 2. **Multi-Model AI Voice**
While MeetGeek uses one AI model, AI Note Taker could offer:
- Choice of voice (different TTS engines)
- Choice of personality (Claude, GPT, Gemini for agent brain)
- Voice cloning for custom agents

### 3. **Collaborative Mock Interviews**
Combine Duo feature with Mock Interview Library:
- Friend + AI conduct mock interview together
- Friend observes, AI asks questions
- Unique hybrid experience
